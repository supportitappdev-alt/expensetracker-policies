# Delete your ExpenseTracker account

You can permanently delete your ExpenseTracker account and all data linked to it.

## Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

1. Open **ExpenseTracker**.
2. Go to **Settings** and scroll to the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete account**, type **DELETE**, and confirm with your Google account.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

- your account (Firebase user, name and email),
- all expenses, categories, subscriptions, money lent/borrowed, budgets, goals, settings, your activity summary and policy acceptance stored on our servers,
- the app's data on that phone (preferences, detected payments, reminders).

## Option 2 — Without the app

Email **supportitappdev@gmail.com** from the Google account you use in ExpenseTracker, with the subject **"Delete my ExpenseTracker account"**. We will delete the account and all its data within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What is kept

Nothing. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts. Files you exported yourself (CSV/PDF) stay wherever you saved them.
